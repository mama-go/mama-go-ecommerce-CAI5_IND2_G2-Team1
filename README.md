# Mama-Go

E-commerce marketing project for **Mama-Go**, an Egyptian brand of monthly planners and notebooks for busy working moms.
Graduation project, DEPI scholarship: Advanced Marketing Automation with AI (Professional).

## Project Name
Mama-Go: Made for mom. Made for life.

## Project Idea
We built an e-commerce business from scratch: **Mama-Go**, an Egyptian brand of monthly planners and notebooks for busy working moms who carry work, home and family at once.

Starting from nothing, the team:
- **Researched the market:** customer journey, persona, competitors, market gap, SWOT, STP, 4Ps and a customer survey
- **Built the product:** an A5 monthly notebook (64 pages), covers, inside pages, stickers, pricing and bundles (Month EGP 99, 3 months EGP 250, Year EGP 999, sticker add-on EGP 50)
- **Set the marketing strategy:** brand voice, content pillars, hook library, 30-day content calendar, funnel, budget and KPIs
- **Built the sales funnel:** a landing page with cash-on-delivery checkout
- **Set up tracking:** Google Tag Manager, GA4, Meta Pixel and UTM tracking to measure the funnel
- **Applying automation:** a workflow for Meta ads and tracking (following the metrics and supporting decisions) is in progress. Order automation is done: every order is saved to Google Sheets and a confirmation email is sent automatically

## KPIs
Test budget: EGP 1,000 over 14 days (Awareness 500, Consideration 300, Conversion 200). Targets marked * are the team's proposals.

| Stage | KPI | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | CPM | EGP 30 to 80 |
| Awareness | Frequency | Under 3* |
| Consideration | CTR | 1% to 3% |
| Consideration | CPC | EGP 1.5 to 6 |
| Consideration | Landing page view rate | 70% or more* |
| Conversion | Conversion rate | 1% or more |
| Conversion | CPA | Under the margin per order (EGP 34 to 85) |
| Conversion | ROAS | 3x or more |
